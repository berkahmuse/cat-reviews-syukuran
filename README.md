Entry for [Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990) — one funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, under 200 characters.

## The review

> One star. My bowl was empty for four whole minutes this morning. Four. I have filed a formal complaint with the dog. He agrees.

127 characters, one line. The complaint is specific and recognisable (the food bowl — the judge brief's own example), the voice is unmistakably feline (an empty bowl is a four-minute crisis, and the cat escalates it through proper channels), and the sting lands in the last two words: the cat filed its complaint with the dog, and the dog — the cat's supposed rival — agrees with the cat against the human.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
